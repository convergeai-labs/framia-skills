# Framia Video Effect Acceptance Matrix

Use this checklist for baseline and candidate renders. Record concrete timestamps, frame paths, or visible player state.

## Story and identity

- The subject and purpose are understandable without relying on the project title or agent explanation.
- Opening, development, climax, and ending form one emotional arc.
- City, product, person, team, or brand semantics are accurate and not contradicted by generated details.
- Any hypothetical premise is presented as creative media rather than accidental factual evidence.

## Visual continuity

- The same subject retains recognizable body, clothing, color, props, and silhouette across clips.
- Numbers, symbols, hands, faces, balls, devices, and architecture do not visibly mutate.
- No unintended text, fake logos, watermarks, UI, or glyph artifacts appear.
- Adjacent clips connect through motion direction, composition, color/light, match cut, or a deliberate contrast.
- Each shot is readable long enough to understand; the piece is not only unrelated slow-motion beauty shots.

## Cinematic quality

- Camera motion has a clear purpose and does not jitter, warp, or drift without motivation.
- Subject separation, lighting direction, depth, and color treatment stay coherent.
- High-energy moments contain controlled motion blur and do not collapse anatomy or objects.
- The final frame is intentional and holds long enough to land.

## Audio

- BGM exists, is audible, and covers the intended duration without abrupt truncation.
- Major visual cuts align with musical beats or designed sound events.
- Dialogue, foley, crowd, impacts, and ambience are balanced when present.
- No clipping, long accidental silence, duplicated audio, or unrelated model-generated speech is audible.

## Timeline and export

- The editor timeline is non-empty.
- Expected video clips occupy the intended order and duration.
- The audio track is populated and aligned.
- Total timeline duration matches the requirement.
- Aspect ratio and resolution match the target.
- Export completes to a stable resource ID.
- The exported or editor-playback media is inspected at normal speed.

## Runtime health

- No unexpected page errors or unhandled rejections affect editing or playback.
- No failed final-media request or HTTP `>=400` blocks verification.
- Thumbnail, CORS, and temporary preview errors are recorded separately from final-media availability.

## Minimum evidence

- Project ID, target node, candidate node, and final resource ID.
- Opening, every clip boundary, climax, and ending checked.
- At least one normal-speed playback.
- Timeline clip count, audio presence, duration, aspect ratio, and resolution.
- Residual visual defects and untested scope stated explicitly.
